LAYUNIN QUARTERLY SYSTEM AUDIT PROTOCOL
MAINTAINING THE ARCHITECTURE OF SUCCESS

Perform this audit every 90 days to ensure your "Layunin" remains in harmony.

1. CONTENT AUDIT (61.8% Weight)
- Traffic Check: Which 3 Pillar Posts drove the most leads? Update them with new AI screenshots.
- Conversion Check: Is the Lead Magnet conversion rate still above 10%? If not, audit the Landing Page copy.

2. MONETIZATION AUDIT (38.2% Weight)
- Pricing: Does the "Architect" tier pricing still reflect your current authority? Consider a 15% increase every 6 months.
- Affiliates: Audit your top 3 affiliates. Send them a personalized "Elite Vault" update.

3. TECHNICAL AUDIT
- Site Speed: Run a PageSpeed Insight. Ensure the theme is still loading in <1s.
- Links: Verify all "Add to Cart" and "Initiate Protocol" links are active.
- Backups: Verify that the Risk Management Protocol (off-site backups) is functioning.

4. PSYCHOLOGICAL AUDIT
- Alignment: Does your daily schedule still match the "Golden Ratio" (61.8% Deep Work)?
- Purpose: Does your current product roadmap still align with your core "Layunin"?

5. THE ASCENSION REVIEW
- Review your "Wall of Impact." Identify 1 client story to turn into a full video case study for the next launch cycle.
