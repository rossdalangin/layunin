🏛️ PODCAST GUESTING STRATEGY (V2.0 PREMIUM)
AUTHORITY POSITIONING FOR THE MASTER ARCHITECT

### 1. THE "ELITE INSIGHT" PITCH
Don't ask to "talk about your brand." Pitch a specific **Architectural Result**.
- **The Hook:** "How to 3X your agency output without hiring a single new staff member using the 'Layunin AI Protocol'."
- **Target Shows:** Local PH business podcasts (e.g., 'The RJ Ledesma Podcast', 'HustleShare', 'The 80/20 Show').

### 2. THE THREE PILLARS OF THE INTERVIEW
Prepare these three "Aha!" moments for every guest appearance:
- **Moment 1: The Friction.** "Why 'working harder' is actually a structural weakness."
- **Moment 2: The System.** "The 3X Output Framework explained."
- **Moment 3: The Result.** "How a local VA used this to buy back 20 hours a week."

### 3. THE "OFF-AIR" PROTOCOL
- **Step 1:** Give the host "Elite Access" to your Level 2 Vault before the show.
- **Step 2:** Provide a custom URL (e.g., `layunin.com/podcast-name`) for the audience.
- **Step 3:** Offer a "Podcast-Only" 20% discount on the Master Productivity Vault.

### 4. FOLLOW-UP VELOCITY
Within 24 hours of the show airing, tag the host on LinkedIn/IG with a high-contrast quote from the episode. Build the bridge.
