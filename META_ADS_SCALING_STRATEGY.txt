LAYUNIN META ADS SCALING STRATEGY
ARCHITECTING PAID TRAFFIC FOR FILIPINO ACHIEVERS

1. THE "FRICTION-TEST" (PHASE 1: RESEARCH)
- Budget: ₱500/day.
- Objective: Traffic to Lead Magnet Landing Page.
- Creative: Use high-contrast "Problem Section" copy (Information Overload vs. Systems).
- Metric: Target ₱15 - ₱30 per lead.

2. THE "MASTERY-SCALE" (PHASE 2: CONVERSION)
- Retargeting: Create a Custom Audience of people who viewed the Lead Magnet but didn't buy a product.
- Creative: Video showing the "Inside the Elite Vault."
- Objective: Purchase of "The Master Planner" (₱1,499).

3. THE "ARCHITECT" FUNNEL (PHASE 3: HIGH-TICKET)
- Retargeting: Existing customers who have bought a digital product.
- Creative: Personal video from the Founder discussing "1-on-1 Mentorship."
- Objective: Lead Form (Contact Page) for Mentorship inquiries.

4. AUDIENCE TARGETING (LOCALIZED)
- Location: Philippines (Exclude rural areas for Phase 1).
- Interests: Harvard Business Review, Tim Ferriss, LinkedIn, Makati/BGC residential areas.
- Psychographics: "Business Owners," "Freelance Professionals," "AI Enthusiasts."

5. CREATIVE PROTOCOL
- Ad Copy: Use "Elite Lexicon" (Protocol, Architecture, Mastery).
- Visuals: Stick to the Navy/Gold brand palette. Use the "Dual Mode" UI screenshots for a modern tech feel.
