# ELITE 7-DAY GOAL RESET PROTOCOL
## ARCHITECTING YOUR "LAYUNIN" FOR ABSOLUTE MASTERY (V1.0)

Welcome to the Protocol. This is not a "to-do list." This is a structural re-engineering of your life. For the next 7 days, you will follow these steps with 100% commitment to reclaim your time and refocus your ambition.

---

### DAY 0: THE AUDIT OF TRUTH
**Objective:** Facing the friction in your current system.
- **The Task:** Log every 15-minute block of your day in a spreadsheet.
- **The Protocol:** Do not change your behavior yet. Just observe. At 10:00 PM, highlight blocks that were "High Output" (Deep Work) and "Low Value" (Friction).
- **The Insight:** Most professionals lose 40% of their day to context switching and reactive responding.

### DAY 1: ALIGNMENT (THE LAYUNIN CORE)
**Objective:** Defining your North Star.
- **The Task:** If money and time were infinite, what is the ONE impact you would leave on the Philippines or your industry?
- **The Protocol:** Strip away the "shoulds" and "musts" from society. Write down your "Layunin" in exactly 12 words or less.
- **The Action:** Place this sentence where you see it every morning.

### DAY 2: THE 3X OUTPUT FRAMEWORK
**Objective:** Implementing the "Rule of 3".
- **The Task:** Identify the 3 most impactful tasks for tomorrow.
- **The Protocol:** "Deep Work" from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Zero notifications. No phone. No email. No meetings.
- **The Insight:** One hour of deep work is worth four hours of shallow work.

### DAY 3: AI SYSTEMS INTEGRATION
**Objective:** Deploying force-multipliers.
- **The Task:** Identify your most repetitive cognitive task (e.g., meeting summaries, content drafting).
- **The Protocol:** Build a modular AI prompt to handle 80% of this task. Test it three times.
- **The Action:** Save this prompt in your "Elite Vault."

### DAY 4: REVENUE ARCHITECTURE
**Objective:** Shifting from trading time to building assets.
- **The Task:** Map your current income streams.
- **The Protocol:** Identify which ones are scalable (Digital) and which are bottlenecked (Service).
- **The Action:** Outline one "Digital Asset" (e.g., a guide, course, or template) that solves a problem you already fixed for yourself.

### DAY 5: COMMUNITY & NETWORK MASTERY
**Objective:** Building the infrastructure of success.
- **The Task:** Reach out to 3 people who are 2 levels ahead of you.
- **The Protocol:** Offer value first. "I read your latest insight and applied it here [link]. Here is a result I got." No "asks." Just "value."
- **The Insight:** Your net worth is a lagging indicator of your network.

### DAY 6: THE WEEKLY REVIEW PROTOCOL
**Objective:** Standardizing excellence.
- **The Task:** Audit the last 7 days vs. your goals.
- **The Protocol:** What was the #1 distraction? What was the #1 win? What system will you implement to prevent the distraction from returning?
- **The Action:** Schedule your "Day 0" for next week.
