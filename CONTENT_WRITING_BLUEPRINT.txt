LAYUNIN CONTENT WRITING BLUEPRINT
THE ARCHITECTURE OF A VIRAL PILLAR POST

1. THE HOOK (The Display Heading)
- Must address a specific "Friction" (e.g., "The Secret Friction Point Stopping Filipino VAs from Earning ₱200k").

2. THE "LAYUNIN" ALIGNMENT (First 100 Words)
- Connect the topic to the user's deep purpose. Use empathetic but authoritative tone.
- SEO: Include your primary keyword in the first paragraph.

3. THE TABLE OF CONTENTS (Automated)
- The theme automatically generates this. Use H2 and H3 tags to create an elite "Strategic Overview."

4. THE MODULAR FRAMEWORK (The Body)
- Every post must offer a "System." Use a [benefit_list] or [cta_box] to break up long text.
- Use bullet points for readability. Paragraphs should not exceed 3-4 lines.

5. THE LEAD MAGNET INJECTION
- At the 50% mark, insert an image or CTA for the "Elite 7-Day Goal Reset Protocol."

6. THE "FINAL CALL" (The Conclusion)
- Summarize the transformation.
- End with a single, clear CTA (e.g., "Initiate the protocol here: [Link]").

7. METADATA (Customizer Settings)
- Ensure the "Meta Description" in **SEO & Social Media** is updated for every post to maximize click-through rate.
