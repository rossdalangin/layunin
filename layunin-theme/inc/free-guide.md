# 7-Day Goal Reset Guide: Reclaim Your Focus & Momentum

## Day 1: The Clarity Audit
Identify exactly where you are and where you've been drifting.
- **Action:** Write down your top 3 wins and top 3 frustrations from the last 90 days.

## Day 2: Defining Your "Layunin"
Connect with your deeper purpose to fuel long-term motivation.
- **Action:** Draft a 1-sentence mission statement for your life.

## Day 3: Systemizing Your Success
Stop relying on willpower; start relying on systems.
- **Action:** Identify one daily task you can automate or delegate.

## Day 4: The Financial Blueprint
Align your goals with your income needs.
- **Action:** Calculate your "Freedom Number" (monthly expenses + savings goals).

## Day 5: AI & Productivity Tools
Leverage modern tech to do more in less time.
- **Action:** Sign up for an AI tool and use it to outline a project.

## Day 6: Overcoming the Procrastination Loop
Understand the psychology of why you stall.
- **Action:** Use the "5-Minute Rule" on your most avoided task.

## Day 7: Your 90-Day Roadmap
Build a concrete plan for the next three months.
- **Action:** Set 3 specific, measurable milestones for the next 90 days.
