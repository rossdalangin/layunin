LAYUNIN TECH STACK & PH PAYMENT PROTOCOLS
ARCHITECTING THE TRANSACTION LAYER FOR THE PHILIPPINE MARKET

1. THE "ELITE" TECH STACK (MINIMALIST & SCALABLE)
- Website: WordPress + Layunin v9.8 (Masterpiece Edition).
- Hosting: SiteGround or Hostinger (Singapore Servers) for <1s local latency.
- Email Marketing: ConvertKit or MailerLite (High deliverability for PH ISPs).
- Course/Asset Delivery: Thrivecart or Gumroad (for ease) OR MemberPress (integrated into WordPress).

2. LOCAL PAYMENT GATEWAYS (THE GCASH/MAYA FACTOR)
90% of your Filipino customers will prefer non-credit card options.
- Primary Recommendation: PayMongo. Support for GCash, Maya, BillEase (Installments), and local cards via a single API.
- Alternative: Xendit. Excellent dashboard and local support.
- Manual Protocol: Bank Transfer (BPI/BDO) + GCash QR. Use the "Contact" page for manual high-ticket onboarding.

3. INSTALLMENT SYSTEMS (BUY NOW, PAY LATER)
For the ₱15k - ₱49k tiers, installments increase conversion by 40%.
- Integrated BNPL: BillEase (via PayMongo) or Maya Credit.
- Internal Protocol: 3-month payment plan for the "Mastery" tier handled via PayPal/Stripe subscriptions.

4. AUTOMATION & INTEGRATION
- Use Zapier or Pabbly Connect to link PayMongo -> ConvertKit -> WordPress.
- Trigger the "Thank You" protocol immediately upon payment confirmation.

5. TAX & COMPLIANCE (PH CONTEXT)
- Use JuanaTax or Taxumo for automated BIR filing as a "Self-Employed" professional/freelancer.
- Ensure the "Terms" and "Privacy" templates (v9.8) are updated with your local business details.
