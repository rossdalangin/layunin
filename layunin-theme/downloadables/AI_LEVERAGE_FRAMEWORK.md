# AI LEVERAGE FRAMEWORK
## FROM AMBITION TO AUTOMATION: THE 10X PROTOCOL

AI is not a tool; it is an elite digital workforce. This framework teaches you how to manage that workforce.

---

### 1. THE MODULAR PROMPT ARCHITECTURE
Don't just "chat." Architect. Every high-leverage prompt must have:
- **Role:** "Act as a world-class Marketing Strategist."
- **Context:** "We are launching a premium WordPress theme for the PH market."
- **Task:** "Analyze this transcript and extract 5 high-impact content hooks."
- **Constraint:** "Output in Markdown table format."

### 2. THE CONTENT MULTIPLIER PROTOCOL
How to turn ONE pillar post into 30 days of content:
- **Step 1:** Use AI to summarize the pillar post.
- **Step 2:** Prompt AI to generate 5 LinkedIn posts (Insight-based).
- **Step 3:** Prompt AI to generate 10 Twitter/X hooks (Contrarian).
- **Step 4:** Prompt AI to generate 3 scripts for short-form video.

### 3. THE "COG-IN-THE-MACHINE" AUDIT
Identify every task in your business.
- Can a Large Language Model (LLM) do it? (Drafting, Coding, Analysis)
- Can an Automation Tool (Zapier/Make) trigger it? (Lead sorting, Calendar sync)
- **Result:** You become the "Master Architect" overseeing the machine.

### 4. ETHICAL & QUALITY GUARDRAILS
Never post "Raw AI."
- **The Human Delta:** AI provides the 80% foundation; you provide the 20% "Layunin" (Soul, specific PH context, personal experience).
