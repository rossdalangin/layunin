LAYUNIN SITE MAINTENANCE PROTOCOL
ENSURING PERPETUAL PERFORMANCE & ELITE SECURITY

1. CORE UPDATES (QUARTERLY)
- WordPress Core: Always keep updated to the latest version.
- Theme Version: Check your 'style.css'. If a new v9.x is released by Layunin, backup your Customizer settings before updating.
- Plugins: Minimize plugin count. Every extra plugin adds "noise" and potential latency.

2. DATABASE & PERFORMANCE AUDIT
- Optimization: Use a plugin like WP-Optimize once a month to clear revisions and overhead.
- Caching: The theme is optimized for server-side caching (SiteGround/Hostinger). Ensure "Object Caching" is enabled.
- Images: Never upload images over 500KB. Use WebP format for sub-millisecond load times.

3. SECURITY PROTOCOLS
- SSL: Ensure your site is running on HTTPS. This is required for GCash/Maya integrations.
- Backups: Setup daily automated backups (to Google Drive or Dropbox).
- Login: Use a "Limit Login Attempts" plugin to prevent brute-force attacks on your elite assets.

4. CUSTOMIZER AUDIT
- Every 90 days, walk through the **Elite Design System** to ensure your branding still reflects your market position.
- Refresh the "Wall of Impact" with the 3 most recent success stories.
